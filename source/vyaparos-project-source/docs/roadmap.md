# Roadmap

## v0.1 (decisions D21 to D54)
Release in three steps:
1. **Usable invoicing:** T0 to T5 (spike, data layer, shell and master data, purchases and consignment, sales and print/PDF, payments and artisan payouts).
2. **Safe and reportable:** T6 backup and restore, T6A Admin Lab, T7 Tally Sales export.
3. **Team and sync:** T8 staff and PIN, T9 sync core, T10 cloud relay, T11 same-WiFi, T12 polish and performance test.

## Open decisions (owner)
- D33 counter mode; D34 barcode and QR; D44 Google Drive backup earlier.
- GST scheme (regular or composition), and the CA questions in docs/ca-questions.md.

## Later candidates (not decided, not started)
- v0.2: Receipt and Purchase Tally vouchers, daily tasks and approvals, returns and credit notes, expenses and day close, stock transfers and stock counts, price lists, courier shipments, optional at-rest encryption.
- v0.3: Google Drive backup, dashboards, WhatsApp helpers, marketplace imports, GST e-invoicing, more invoice templates, iPhone support.

## Not planned
Cloud-first SaaS, Tally replacement, native apps, payroll or HR, bank integrations.
